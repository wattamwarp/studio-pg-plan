Four options on one idea: let the corridor take the slant

Once the Sikandar side can take rooms on a 1′-0″ setback, three of the four boundaries can carry a window and a double-loaded corridor becomes possible — which is what makes a proper plan work here. The corridor follows the garden boundary, so the garden band stays a constant depth and every one of those rooms is identical. All the taper is pushed into the Sikandar band, and the core drops into its narrow rear end, where the band is too shallow for a room anyway.

The four options are the same idea tuned four ways — more rooms, bigger rooms, or a deeper garden band. Pick by what you want to sell.

How to choose

Option A is the one I would build — eight rooms with the bedrooms still in the high seventies, and the Sikandar rooms coming out as the best on the floor.

Option B adds a ninth room for four extra units across a G+3, at the cost of dropping every garden bedroom to about 64 sq ft.

Option C is the four-wide-garden-rooms version you asked for. The garden bedrooms grow to roughly 98 sq ft, which is the only option where a twin room feels genuinely comfortable — but it costs a unit.

Option D pushes the garden band to 19 ft so those bedrooms get a full 11 ft of depth, and pays for it by taking the balconies off the Sikandar rooms.
